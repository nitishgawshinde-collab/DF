Interconnection

How the workflows connect

Embedded workflows turn uploads into verified evidence in HIVE. Business processes consume that evidence, go through Luxembourg Tax, and end in OBS. Here the lanes are platform layers; each box is coloured by the team that owns it.

  • Upload once, reuse many times. Propose evidence only when document type, ownership, jurisdiction, validity, current replacement state, access and process rules permit. Complete is the default; Incomplete requires an explicit approved exception (D-04). Rejected evidence is never eligible.
  • Missing evidence goes back through intake. If a BCTS or Tax Relief request is blocked, the missing document is uploaded and verified through an embedded workflow, then linked to the case by its exact Document ID. Upload may start from the case screen but must use the shared intake controls.
  • Milestones are independent. Document Complete, Tax Approved, Account Available and OBS Implemented remain independent. On resumption, check freshness and material changes; whether unchanged approval can proceed without human reapproval remains D-26.
  • Termination selects one eligible service. Use the authoritative service register. Eligibility of existing or migrated services, and the source of that register, require business confirmation; a DocuFlow New Business case is not assumed mandatory.

FR-META-02; FR-INT-02–04; FR-BP1-06–08; D-01 / D-24 / D-26. Revalidation (FR-RET-01) checks the whole package, not only edited answers: evidence expiry, replacement or status change, access and ownership changes (including iCRM reallocation), and changes to the applicable rules, DMN or form version. If nothing changed, the request returns straight to Tax; otherwise allocation and requirements re-run and the user reconfirms evidence. After an account wait the same checks run (FR-RET-02): a stale approval returns to Tax; expired, replaced or inaccessible evidence returns to Front Office. Missing evidence resumes this same case.

  • Returns stay on the same case. Evidence and history remain linked; resubmission revalidates the whole package (answers, evidence expiry and replacement, access, applicable rules) before returning to Luxembourg Tax.
  • CDDM Poland can send back to Tax when an approved instruction cannot be implemented in OBS.
  • Another country opens a new linked case: Account and client data carry over; rates, dates, declarations and evidence do not.
  • Account not yet in OBS: the approved outcome waits in interim storage until BECOM sends Account Created, then correlation, current approval and evidence freshness gates determine whether implementation may resume.
  • Open D-01: BTA1–BTA9 rules and who may override (OTL proposed).

Required controls · shared by all applicable paths

Lifecycle and exception handling

These controls apply alongside the main swimlanes. A successful human decision alone does not establish evidence eligibility, target-system completion or archival.

LifecycleExecution and exit conditionRequirements / decisions
Deficiency and remediationAuthorized reviewer records deficiency, reason and accountable remediator → remediation task → remediator proposes resolution → authorized acceptance or permitted waiver → recompute document and case eligibility. Failed acceptance returns work; unresolved blockers remain visible. Rejected is not successful completion.FR-DEF-01–03; D-21
Replacement and evidence impactCreate a new Document ID linked to its predecessor → validate and obtain all required acceptance, including Tax where required → atomically change the current reference. Until acceptance, the existing accepted document remains current. Preserve the exact evidence used historically. Replacement, expiry, revocation or context change triggers impact assessment, notification and configured reassessment. Resolve competing successors explicitly.FR-REP-01–08; D-15, D-16
Independent milestonesTrack documentary outcome, Tax approval, account availability and implementation independently. Preserve prior decisions when current progression is invalidated or reopened. Display present validity, owner, blocker and next action separately from historical completion.FR-STA-03–04; D-22
Implementation and effectivenessApproved instruction → authorized input → independent check under agreed policy → authoritative result and reconciliation → implementation complete. Partial failure stays open with an exception owner. Future-dated activation/termination is Scheduled until its effective date; cancellation and backdating follow an approved policy. Termination affects exactly the selected service.FR-INT-04; FR-BP1-06–08; D-09, D-20, D-24
Completion and archivalAll required case gates pass → Completed → authorized archive action/policy → Archived. Apply access, retention and legal-hold controls. Restoration and eventual disposal require approved rules and audit. Rejected closure remains visibly distinct from successful completion.FR-ARC-01–04; D-25
